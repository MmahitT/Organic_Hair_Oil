## 2. Dependency audit

`npm audit` (2026-11-11, after the fixes below): **0 critical, 0 high**, 7 moderate; **production dependencies: 0 vulnerabilities**.

- Fixed: `tinypool` (critical, test-runner dependency) and `@vitest/mocker` (moderate) by upgrading Vitest 3 → 4.1.12; `sharp` (high, dev-only image tool) 0.33 → 0.35.5.
- Remaining ⚠️: all 7 moderate findings are **one** nested `minimatch` (denial of service through crafted glob patterns) inside the TypeScript-ESLint lint tooling. It is dev-only, never shipped, and only reachable with attacker-controlled glob input, which we do not have. `npm audit fix`/an override did not resolve it in this environment; re-check when `@typescript-eslint` next releases.

## 3. Data privacy & retention (owner decisions needed)

What we store: orders (name, phone, address, optional email), payment claims (transaction ID/reference), contact messages, reviews (hashed phone), customer notes/blocks, audit log, optional customer accounts.
Proposed retention (to be confirmed by the owner and reflected in the Privacy Policy): orders and payment records — as long as accounting/legal obligations require; contact messages — 12 months; audit log — 24 months. No automatic deletion job exists yet; deletion on request is done by the owner/admin. Phone numbers are masked in public views and logs.

## 4. Residual risks and deployment items

- ⏭️ **Hosting (Phase 11):** serve the website with `client/dist/_headers` (CSP etc.), enable HTTPS/HSTS, set `TRUST_PROXY` correctly behind the host, keep the site and API on the same registrable domain (cookies), restrict MongoDB Atlas to the API's addresses, back up the database, store secrets in the host's secret manager.
- ⚠️ **Rate-limit counters are in memory** (fine for one server). With several API instances, move them to a shared store (Redis) — flagged for deployment.
- ⚠️ **Admin accounts have no two-factor sign-in.** Mitigations: 5-failure lockout, 8/15-minute IP limit, short-lived tokens, full audit log. Recommended future improvement.
- ⚠️ **Order confirmation tokens** (24 h) travel in the URL fragment/query so a customer can reopen their order page; they are scrubbed from logs, `Referrer-Policy` is `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, and they grant only the masked order view.
- ⚠️ **Sign-up reveals whether a phone/email is already registered** (409). Accepted by the spec; customers rarely register (guest checkout).
- ⚠️ **No automated penetration test by a third party.** This review is the project's own; a professional test before launch is recommended.
